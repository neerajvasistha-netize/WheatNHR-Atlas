# GWAS MTAs and known genes from the Saini et al. 2022 supplementary file

Source: 11032_2022_1282_MOESM1_ESM.xls, supplementary of Saini DK, Chahal A, Pal N, Srivastava P, Gupta PK (2022)
"Meta-analysis reveals consensus genomic regions associated with multiple disease resistance in wheat" (Molecular Breeding).
Only original GWAS MTAs (Online Resource 6) and known genes (Online Resource 7) are used, credited to their own papers.
The meta-QTLs and the QTL list (Online Resource 2, genetic positions only, no markers) are not added.

Added (seed_ad77.json, 42 records, grade C):
- MTAs: Gerard et al. 2017 (STB, 13), Muqaddasi et al. 2019 (STB, 7), Gupta et al. 2019 (Karnal bunt, 7),
  Singh et al. 2020 (Karnal bunt, 6), Hu et al. 2020 (FHB, 7).
- Genes: Stb8 (7B, Adhikari et al. 2003) and Snn6 (6A, Gao et al. 2015).
Skipped: papers already in the atlas (Kidane 2017, Alemu 2021, Dutta 2021, Phan 2018, AlTameemi 2021, Emebiri 2019,
Tessmann 2018); Mahboubi 2021 (preprint, no published version identified), Holder 2018 (thesis), Shi 2021 (paper not
identified), Larkin 2020 (no marker names); genes already in the atlas; functional defence genes (UGTs, WRKY, chitinase,
etc.), which are not resistance loci.
Positions: published physical position of the marker from our marker table where available, otherwise the bp value in
the supplement. Hu et al. Excalibur_c22724_85 was dropped (62.22 Mb in the supplement, a typo for ~622 Mb).
