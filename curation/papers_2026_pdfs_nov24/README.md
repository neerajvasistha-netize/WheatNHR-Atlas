# 2026 papers read from full-text PDFs (seed_ad88)

The owner uploaded 32 PDFs (Paper_2026.zip) on 24 Nov 2026 for the papers that the first 2026 pass could not read
(see `../papers_2026_nov24/`). PDFs are not kept here.

## Added: 160 records from 11 papers (`records88.py`, `build88.py`, `insilico.py`)
- Positions read from the papers' tables, with the assembly each paper states (grade A for peaks, B for intervals):
  Zhang et al. (Gansu landraces, stripe rust), Bian et al. (FHB), Wang et al. (yellow rust multi-environment QTL),
  Luo et al. (stripe rust), Jan et al. (stripe rust), Thapa et al. (bacterial leaf streak), Xiang et al. (Yr52 and QTL),
  Zhuo et al. (Pm4 allele), Thielmann et al. (powdery mildew colony loci, control and primed).
- Placed in silico from published marker positions because the paper gives only cM (grade C):
  Thirunarayanan et al. (spot blotch, 74 of 103 markers placed) and Korra et al. (Exserohilum spike blight, 14 of 24).
  Korra et al. list candidate genes at Mb values equal to the cM values, so those genes were not used.
- Exserohilum spike blight is a new disease in the atlas (necrotroph group).
